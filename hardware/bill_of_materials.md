## Economic Viability Comparison
Compared to commercial vision-guided robotic sorting installations that typically exceed $50,000–$100,000 USD in capital investment, EcoSort demonstrates that autonomous 4-DOF multi-material segregation is viable at decentralized municipal collection points for under **$50 USD**.
